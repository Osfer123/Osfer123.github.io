I study organized crime, migration, and violence with computational and statistical methods.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Sociology at the University of California, Davis. I received my Ph.D. in Sociology from the University of California, Los Angeles.

My research examines how criminal networks evolve, how criminal organizations produce and reproduce violence, and the risks migrants face while crossing contested territories. I also use spatial statistical methods to study the geographies of labor migration in the United States.
